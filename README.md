## About Freetower

Freetower is a seven-civilization building and open-world creation game designed around Bitcoin-native identity.

The long-term premise is simple: start from an ancient mythological civilization and build the future that only your civilization could create. The seven civilizations share a common foundation of survival, production, construction, exploration, technology, and era progression, while each follows its own cultural and mythological direction.

## Current Development

Freetower is currently in the planning stage. Development tasks are being planned, created, scoped, and sequenced before the next implementation phase begins.

All development code currently in this repository has been produced by Codex and has no practical application value.

## Civilization Evolution

Every civilization is intended to move through a shared sequence of eras:

```text
Ancient Age
  ↓
Tribal Age
  ↓
City-State / Kingdom Age
  ↓
Industrial Age
  ↓
Modern Technology Age
  ↓
Future Civilization Age
  ↓
Mythic Technology Apex
```

An era change is not only a numeric upgrade. Over time it should change architecture, roads, lighting, transportation, production facilities, NPC appearance, music, environment, technology, and city scale.

> Technology should not erase a civilization's culture. It should allow that culture to evolve.

## World Structure

### Personal Civilization Territory

Each player has a stable territory for survival, gathering, construction, production, exploration, technology research, ruins, NPC quests, and civilization growth. Long-term personal construction is not intended to be subject to unrestricted destruction by other players.

### Civilization Shared World

Players of the same civilization will eventually share public spaces for civilization wonders, ports, research, civilization quests, fleets, large mythic events, and structured rankings.

### Freetower World

The Freetower stands in the central sea between the seven civilizations. Early players can see it, but cannot enter it. It is the central landmark, a shared mystery, a long-term objective, and a planned gateway to later multiplayer content.

When opened, the Tower may host cross-civilization social spaces, multiplayer tasks, player spaces, AI NPCs, world events, cross-civilization markets, player creation, and approved Ordinals asset interactions. Those are planned systems, not current features.

## Multiplayer Civilization Systems

**Planned / long-term:**

- Public construction and civilization wonders
- Shared research and public ports
- Fleet building and civilization events
- Mythical creature encounters
- Building, exploration, technology, trade, sailing, and creation rankings
- Time-limited challenges

Competition is intended to be structured and seasonal, without allowing unrestricted destruction of personal settlements.

## Trade & Ocean Exploration

**Planned:**

```text
Nearshore Exploration
  ↓
Cross-Civilization Routes
  ↓
Civilization Fleets
```

The long-term system may include small boats, salvage, islands, sea monsters, ports, shipping, escort missions, fleet building, naval conflict, NPC caravans, player orders, civilization orders, rare resources, trade routes, and trade reputation.

Freetower does not need to issue a new game token for trade.

## Civilization Competition

**Long-term vision:**

1. Trade War
2. Technology Race
3. Cultural Competition
4. Military Conflict

Military conflict is intended for oceans, resource islands, independent battle zones, shipping routes, naval strongholds, and world events. The format should be season-based, match-based, or event-based—not an always-on permission to erase another player's capital.

## Opening the Freetower

Opening the Tower is intended to be a world event rather than a simple player-level reward. The long-term conditions may include:

- Seven civilizations reaching required stages
- Civilization ports and public fleets being completed
- Trade routes being established
- World missions being completed
- Tower relics being collected
- Tower energy being restored
- A shared opening event completed by all seven civilizations

## Freetower Multiplayer World

The opened Tower is a planned shared world for tower floors, cross-civilization activities, social spaces, player-created spaces, world events, and future creator systems. It is not open in the current local prototypes.

## AI Creation Ecosystem

**Planned / long-term:** AI may help players create equipment, items, NPCs, quests, rooms, maps, events, and gameplay rules.

AI must not generate uncontrolled executable code or write authoritative game state directly. The intended pipeline is:

```text
Player Idea
  ↓
AI Structured Draft
  ↓
Rule Validation
  ↓
Balance Validation
  ↓
Safety Validation
  ↓
Player Revision
  ↓
Review
  ↓

```

## Contribution

Contribution is a long-term identity-linked recognition system, not a token. It is not tradable or transferable and should come from auditable contribution events such as civilization construction, world events, player-created gameplay, quests, items, guides, adopted proposals, and verified community value.

Likes alone do not generate Contribution. Private messages and normal chat do not generate Contribution. AI cannot independently decide Contribution.

## On-chain vs Game Server

Bitcoin and other chains are suitable for ownership, asset identity, scarcity, important credentials, and public proofs.

The game server is suitable for position, combat, building progress, quest state, cooldowns, rankings, chat, temporary resources, and real-time gameplay.

Bitcoin manages identity, ownership, and important proofs. The game server manages high-frequency gameplay. Freetower will not attempt to write every movement, attack, or building action to Bitcoin.
